# QUALITY OF LIFE — research

These are the research notes for the Quality of Life tree, as of 2026-11-20 (Charting the Unknown, patches through 2.04). Every figure names its basis: **patch** notes, **guide** text, a guide's in-game **screenshot**, or a **player** report. Each plate's Remedy shows the same basis. Plates that take their how-to from Mounts or Riches (`base`) inherit those plates' research; see `docs/RICHES-RESEARCH.md` and the Mounts plates' sources.
