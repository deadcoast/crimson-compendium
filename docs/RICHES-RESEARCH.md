# RICHES — research

These are the research notes for the Riches tree, as of 2026-11-19 (game version 2.04, Charting the Unknown). Every figure names its source. Figures from player reports are marked as such: one player's result, not measured.

The starting state is: main story finished (Journey's End), Charting the Unknown owned but not started.

## Currencies and exchange

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| 100 Copper = 1 Silver; 500 Silver = 1 Gold Bar | thegameswiki, *How to Make Money Fast* |
| Since 2.01, donating Silver to camp gives ×10 Camp Funds (it was 1:1) | Vulkk, *Update 2.01* |
| One Hernand Bond = 10 Gold Bars; investing in bonds takes 10 Bonds (100 Gold Bars) | consolepulse, *Best Ways to Make Silver and Gold Bars* |
| Ships, facility leases, church donations and dispatch fees are paid in Camp Funds | outputlag; players.com.ua; Vulkk |
| Derived: 1 Gold Bar → 500 Silver → 5,000 Camp Funds via donation | sum of the two rows above |

## Greymanes (dispatch)

- **Golden Beer** permanently doubles dispatch rewards, and you only need to hold it. It comes from the Byron's House chain and the boss Mazzul at Burnhand Quarry. Sources: TheGamer (via Pulse), thegameswiki *Golden Beer*, games.gg.
- **Church donations.** Donating Camp Funds raises dispatch rewards in that church's region, up to +200%, separately for each region. Sources: players.com.ua; GamesRadar (player reports). Each donation level adds 2%, per consolepulse *Passive Income*. Church and Beer stack multiplicatively, per games.gg.
- **Estate security** (Hillside, Oakenshield, Azerian and Sungrove manors) pays Camp Funds, per consolepulse *Passive Income*. Player reports: 20k–30k+ per dispatch once stacked (GamesRadar), and 5–8× the base with church, Beer and Repeat (outputlag).
- **Storage dispatches** produce goods you sell for Silver: carpets at Cowbell Ranch and wine at Bluemont Cask House (consolepulse).
- **Grey-0 mecha workers** fill routine slots. They also drain Food and Funds on an unprofitable queue (consolepulse).
- **Gold Bar Investment Mission** (added in 2.01) costs 1.9–3.1M Camp Funds and needs an Accountant plus 3 members, for 3 days (Vulkk). One player paid 2.4M for four bars (outputlag). Classed as a **trap**.

## Real estate (Charting the Unknown)

- **Facilities.** There are 18, in four types: Sawmill, Restaurant, Smithy and Tailor's Shop (Vulkk, *Facility Management*). Leasing unlocks through *Renewed Vitality* / *The Stilled Saw*: the Timberham Sawmill notice on the Hernand board (games.gg; Neon Lights).
  - **Lease cost:** 15,000. Xbox Play says Camp Funds; Vulkk says Copper.
  - **Running it:** 4 Greymanes with Logging/Crafting level 3 for the sawmill (Xbox Play); keep prices low while levelling popularity, then raise them at max level (Vulkk).
  - **Player reports:** about 1M Camp Funds a day from one shop; a sawmill taking a player from about 30k to nearly 10M in a couple of hours (GamesRadar).
- **Castles.** Drakesfall Castle and Flame Knights' Castle can be claimed after the main story. Flame Knights' needs 20 comrades, one with Construction, and the rebuild takes 15 in-game days (Noobfeed). Castles gather merchants (Mobalytics). No source reports castle income.

## Ships (Charting the Unknown)

- **Prices in Camp Funds:** Humble Rowboat/Bark 2,000 · Small Hernandian 40,000 · Medium 1,200,000 · Large 6,000,000 (outputlag, Playnews, GamesRadar).
- **Where to get one:** buy from fleetmasters at Vellua (Hopkins), Demeniss and Delesyia. Or build through the shipbuilding dispatch at Pailune, which costs fewer funds and more materials (outputlag).
- **Crew roles:** First Mate, Rigger, Lookout, Carpenter, Gunner (needed for cannons) and Deckhand (needed for island dispatch). Roles can only be changed in port (outputlag).
- **Maritime trade:** load goods at one port and sell at another. Ships are formally a mount type (thegameswiki *Ships*; outputlag).

## Islands (Charting the Unknown)

- **Pioneering:** 3 comrades assigned as Deckhands, on a 12-hour dispatch with Kliff and the ship inside the circle (Whisper of the House; GameRant; camzillasmom).
- A pioneered island gets an anchorage, ship teleportation, and new missions and shops (camzillasmom; Whisper of the House).
- **Islands named so far:** Auronui (first), Samora (pirates, *Outlaws of the Ocean*) and Winding (*Secret of the Winding Island*) (outputlag).
- At launch, the dispatch stalled at 100%. Patch 2.04.02 added a pioneering prompt but didn't say the stall was fixed (thegameswiki *Charting the Unknown*).
- **Gap:** no source yet documents island upgrade tiers or island income figures.

## Pailune

- **Reconstructing Pailune** follows Chapter 7 and needs camp level 2. You donate materials; the rebuild takes about 2 in-game days and 6 hours (Playnews; thegameswiki).
- **Moving the camp to Pailune** is the final camp upgrade. It needs the main story, every *Scattered Embers* quest and a fully upgraded Howling Hill (Nerd's Chalk). Recruits then build the Pailune Council, Pailune Institute and more (Epic Games Store guide).

## Trade

- **Patch 1.16** added 133 Trading Posts, 28 trade goods, 7 Wagon Workshops and a Trade map tab. Royal Trading Post prices were quadrupled. Prices drop when one good floods a market, and more cargo is lost when a wagon is destroyed or retrieved (consolepulse).
- **Wagons:** recruit Brice (*A Rumor in Glenbright Farm*) and an Engineer, then build the Timberturner Wainwright (consolepulse). Carl packages trade goods for 100 Copper per unit (Vulkk).
- **Volworks Armory weapons run** (Demeniss), player reports: 1,200 boxes paid about 20M and 300 boxes about 5M (≈16,700 per box), on about a 20-minute round trip (outputlag).

## Bank

- **Gold Bar investment** needs a 100-Silver Strongbox permit. It offers Low, Medium and High risk, settling about every 3 in-game days (thegameswiki). Patch 1.16 capped gains and losses at 40 bars per cycle (consolepulse).
- **Hernand Bonds** see Currencies above. Crude Gold Bars can't fund investments or Bonds (consolepulse).
- The **Golden Piggy Bank** makes Copper Pouches while you're offline (consolepulse).

## Player control

- **Golden Apple + 3 Gold Ore → Gold Bar** (500 Silver). The recipe is at the Witch's Hideout south of Pailune; hedgehogs that drop apples are near Grey Rock Dock (consolepulse). Patch 2.04 reduced hedgehog spawns (outputlag).
- **Pickpocketing at Demeniss Wildlife Park:** one player took 6 Gold Bars in about 25 minutes (GamesRadar).
- **Crude Gold Bar circuit:** 5 caches, about 400 bars, at 5 Silver each ≈ 2,000 Silver (consolepulse). outputlag values harpy-lair crude bars at about 500 Silver each. The sources conflict.
- **Duo and Five-Card:** Tommaso Duo has a 300-Silver buy-in and Beighen Five-Card 150. A Five-Card session returns 400–600 Silver. A Cheat ability unlocks after you watch a cheater three times (thegameswiki). At one point the Tommaso Duo NPC did not spawn (Mythras).
