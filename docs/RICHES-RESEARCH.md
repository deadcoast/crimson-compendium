# RICHES — research

These are the research notes for the Riches tree, as of 2026-11-19 (patch 2.04.02, Charting the Unknown). Every figure names its basis: **patch** notes, **guide** text, a guide's in-game **screenshot**, or a **player** report (one player's result, not measured). The app shows the same basis on every plate's ledger.

The starting state is: main story finished (Journey's End), Charting the Unknown owned but not started.

## Currencies and exchange

| Fact | Basis · source |
|---|---|
| 100 Copper = 1 Silver; a bank pays 500 Silver per Gold Bar, a merchant about 190 | guide · The Games Wiki, Console Pulse |
| Since 2.01, donated Silver gives ×10 Camp Funds; before 2.01 it was 1:1 | patch · Vulkk *Update 2.01*; The Games Wiki *Patch 2.01.00* |

| Ships, leases, Blessings and dispatch fees are paid in Camp Funds | guide · Playnews, The Games Wiki |

## Greymanes (dispatch)

- **Golden Beer** doubles the main reward of every dispatch while held (Camp Funds, food, timber…), on every repeat cycle; special drops are excluded. A security run went from about 7,000 to about 14,000. Route: Chapter 8, the Demeniss noble houses in order through House Elmore, then House Byron's *Dark Justice* (*A Secret Request*, *One Step Ahead*) to Mazzul at Burnhand Quarry. Basis: guide · The Games Wiki, FDayTalk, TheGamer.
- **Church Blessings** add 2% per level to dispatch rewards in that region, up to +200%, permanently. About 110,000 buys +100% in Hernand and about 150,000 in Demeniss. They multiply with Golden Beer. Basis: guide · The Games Wiki, Console Pulse.
- **Estate Security** pays Camp Funds for food. In Console Pulse's screenshots, Azerian Manor paid 13K in 7 hours and Hillside Manor 1.8K in 17 hours. The Games Wiki records about 30,000 on a buffed first run and about 15,000 on auto-repeat because of a bug.
- **Excavation**: 37,000 Camp Funds per Tinkerton completion and 20,000 at Acroser Village. Patch 1.06 made stronghold excavations repeatable, and Explorer slots cap at five. Basis: guide · The Games Wiki.
- **Supply dispatches** (quarries, fishing docks, logging, farming) pay food and materials. Examples: large-scale logging about 16,000 wood per 18–24 hours; cacao about 17,000 provisions with eight workers. Basis: guide · The Games Wiki, Console Pulse.
- **Grey-0 workers**: the Marne Steel Armory mission needs an Engineer. It returns 1–3 workers every 3 hours and costs 5,000 weapons, 1,000 wood, 2,000 provisions and 10,000 Silver per run. Basis: guide · The Games Wiki. Console Pulse warns that workers make a losing queue lose faster.
- **Time**: every timer runs on the in-game clock. Sleeping or waiting at a campfire advances it, and fatigue gates sleep. Basis: guide · The Games Wiki; screenshot · Console Pulse.

## Real estate (Charting the Unknown, 2.04)

- **Facilities**: 18 in four types — Sawmill, Restaurant, Smithy, Tailor's Shop. Basis: guide · Vulkk.
  - A lease needs a liberated stronghold, its regional faction quest finished, an established camp and the lease fee. Basis: guide · NeonLights.
  - Staff by type: Cooking at the Vellua restaurant, Smithing at the Zargan Tankworks smithy, Tailoring, Woodworking at Timberham. Basis: guide · NeonLights.
  - **Lease: 15,000.** Xbox Play says Camp Funds; Vulkk says Copper.
  - Unlock: the Timberham notice on the Hernand board (*The Stilled Saw* → *The Saw Turns Again*, which ends at Popularity Level 3). Basis: guide · Xbox Play, GAMES.GG.
- **Screenshot figures** from a young Timberham sawmill:
  - 48,422 earned over 40 hours open, on 4,200 daily wages (Xbox Play).
  - 18,094 earned in 7 hours, against 8,500 wages and 25,160 daily upkeep (NoobFeed).
- **Player report**: about 1M Camp Funds a day from one shop (GamesRadar). NeonLights notes that **patch 2.04.02 fixed a bug inflating facility earnings**, so figures from before October 30 overstate the real rate.
- **Castles**: both need Journey's End, 20 comrades (one with Construction) and 15 in-game days.
  - Drakesfall Castle: *Shield of Hernand*, then 30,000 funds.
  - Flame Knights' Castle: Damiane's *From the Ashes* through *Path of the Politician*, then 100,000 funds, 10,000 weapons, 10,000 provisions, 40,000 stone and 40,000 wood.
  - No source reports castle income. Basis: guide · NoobFeed, GamingBolt.

- Small Shipbuilding takes five comrades, 12 in-game hours, and 600 / 600 / 1,200 / 10,000 of the listed materials. Basis: screenshot · Whisper of the House.

## Islands

- **Auronui** is pioneered from its Anchorage: keep the ship inside the marked area, open Map → Facilities and start Pioneer with **three comrades**. After the 12-hour dispatch, return to the same Anchorage and choose Complete Mission. Rewards include the Forgotten Treasure Map and the Ship Teleportation Device blueprint. Basis: guide and screenshot · Whisper of the House.
- Islands after Auronui: Samora (pirates) and Winding Island.
- **Gap**: no source documents island upgrade tiers or island income.

## Pailune

- **Reconstruction** follows Chapter 7 and needs camp level 2; the rebuild takes about 2 in-game days and 6 hours. Basis: guide · Playnews.
- **The move** (*The Return Home*, from Serkis in Hernand Town) needs:
  - the main story and Epilogue;
  - at least 31 Scattered Embers and 37 Grounds of the Sunrise quests;
  - a rebuilt Pailune and a fully upgraded Howling Hill.

  It doubles farm and ranch space and gives 1,000 Private Storage slots (+360). Make a manual save first; the cutscene overwrites the autosave. Basis: guide · Gaming ProMax; the *Proud Returnee* achievement confirms the relocation exists (TrueAchievements).

## Trade

- **Patch 1.16** added:
  - 133 Trading Posts;
  - 28 trade goods (24 high-value, 4 regular);
  - seven Wagon Workshops;
  - a Trade map tab with live prices;
  - supply pressure that lowers a price you keep selling into;
  - Royal post prices ×4, with wagons that stay after a Royal delivery.

  It also raised the cargo lost when a wagon is destroyed or retrieved. Basis: patch · Console Pulse.
- Wagons need Brice (*A Rumor in Glenbright Farm*), an Engineer and the Timberturner Wainwright. Basis: guide · Console Pulse.
- **A published run** (screenshots · Whisper of the House):
  - 150 Packaged Cheese bought at 118 (17,700 Silver), for Varnia's Royal post, which listed Cheese at 1,000.
  - 150 Packaged Salt bought 22% below reference and sold at 563 each: 84,450 Silver.

## Bank

- A 100-Silver Strongbox permit opens Gold Bar investment, which settles about every 3 in-game days. Basis: guide · The Games Wiki.
  - Low risk: 0–2%.
  - Medium: 15–20%.
  - High: 50–55%, and it can lose bars.
- Patch 1.16 capped gains and losses at 40 bars per cycle. Basis: patch · Console Pulse.
- Crude Gold Bars cannot be invested.
- Patch 2.01 added a **Gold Bar Investment dispatch mission**. No retrievable source documents its cost or payout, so the app does not route through it.

## Player control

- **Gold Bar crafting**: 1 Golden Apple + 3 Gold Ore at a Witch. The recipe is on the bookshelf of the Witch's Hideout south of Pailune. Golden Apple hedgehogs appear near Grey Rock Dock, the Eastern Hills of Delesyia and Tariv, roughly 16:00 to midnight; patch 2.04 reduced their spawns. A blacksmith's 100 Gold Ore → 1 bar is over thirty times worse. Basis: guide · Console Pulse, Method.
- **Pickpocketing nobles** at Demeniss Wildlife Park: one player took about 6 bars in 25 minutes (player · GamesRadar). Console Pulse reports 2 bars per optimised run.
- **Crude Gold circuit**: about 400 bars across five caches at 5 Silver each — about 2,000 Silver. The Harpy Nest alone holds about 150. Basis: guide · Console Pulse.
- **Duo and Five-Card**: buy-ins of 15 (Hernand), 150 (Beighen, Five-Card) and 300 Silver (Tommaso). A winning Five-Card session pays 400–600 Silver (guide · The Games Wiki). NeonLights reports that the AI calls reckless opening all-ins. Patch 2.03.01 fixed the missing Tommaso dealers (Mythras).
