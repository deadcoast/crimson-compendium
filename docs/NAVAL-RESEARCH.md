## Conflicts (shown on the plates)

- **Where a legendary's materials must be.** KeenGamer: in your inventory. FinalBoss: Private Storage, and in the bag the build stays unavailable.
- **Where the Phantom Ship is built.** Stoneward Harbor (KeenGamer), Port Delesyia (Gaming Deputy), any port (Game8).
- **Where the Assault Ship is built.** Delesyia (KeenGamer), any port (FinalBoss).
- **The pearl dispatch's rate.** About 1 run in 5 (FinalBoss) against a set number every 10 in-game hours (Gaming Deputy). Neither is measured.
- **Iliya's pioneering cost.** Four comrades and 18 hours (FinalBoss) against Auronui's three and 12 (Whisper of the House); islands may differ.
- **Sea Fan Coral's look.** A white-ish plant (Vulkk) against low dark fan clumps (KeenGamer).
- **Ambergris's name.** FinalBoss writes "Amber Grease"; the in-game build menu (KeenGamer's capture) reads *Ambergris*. The Materials tree's *Amberress* was a misreading and is corrected.

## Gaps

- No published picture of Golden Coral, Giant Jellyfish Skin or an Ambergris node. Those plates show the ship each builds and the build menu that lists it.
- No hold size for the Large Greymane or any legendary.
- No profit figure for any sea trade route.
- Nothing says what the Phantom, Catfish and Assault hunts reward beyond the blueprint.
